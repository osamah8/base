# Guidance

Official guidance to follow. The GOV.UK writing guidance below comes from the base and is the house style for every project. A project adds its own guidance documents in this folder, beside these, and lists them in its `PROJECT.md` under "Own guidance".

The binding rules are in `README.md` under "Writing rules". The summary is an agent's reading, dated: for anything that matters, check the full text or the live page. Full-text copies are unchanged wording under the Open Government Licence v3.0; replace them when the source changes, and note the new retrieval date.
